The measures differ by platform, so compare each with its own peers, not across platforms. One bright spot on X: the 17 Nov “One destination. Endless possibilities.” post drew 16K views and 176 interactions (11 per 1,000), the best ratio captured on a large SEVEN post.

Sources: post register (TikTok, YouTube RSS likes); 6 Nov analysis (X and Instagram engagement, 5–15 posts per account); 20 Nov evidence X feed.

X interactions are replies, reposts, likes and bookmarks. On Instagram only likes are public, and they depend on post age. TikTok gives the richest signal and the highest response, so it should get the creative experiments. Read the 17 Nov X post closely: we don’t know if it was boosted, but its engagement ratio is far above the 4 Oct ad-scale post.
