Market · brand hygiene

Small inconsistencies split SEVEN’s reach and blur who it is

Identity

TikTok and Snapchat bios say SEVEN is a Qiddiya company; YouTube still says PIF. There are two Facebook pages, and YouTube’s LinkedIn link goes to an admin-only page.

Hashtags

Variants split each destination: #سڤن_أبها and #سڤن_ابها, tags with hidden right-to-left marks, and tags run into punctuation (#سڤن_تبوك!, #SEVEN_Tabuk,).

YouTube

Uploads went 21 (2023), 4, 2, then 15 (2026). 1.62K subscribers; ad-served films carry the views. No playlists by destination.

Snapchat

A steady 2.7 Spotlights a week, mostly Tabuk. No public view counts, and 13 of the 16 posts captured have no comments.

Sources: post register and public profiles (TikTok, Snapchat, YouTube About), 19–20 Nov 2026.

None of this costs money to fix. One bio line, one Facebook page, a fixed LinkedIn link and a hashtag style sheet (no punctuation inside tags, one spelling per destination) should be done within two weeks. YouTube needs a decision: either a real Shorts cadence fed by TikTok cuts, or treat it as an ad library and stop counting its views as organic.
