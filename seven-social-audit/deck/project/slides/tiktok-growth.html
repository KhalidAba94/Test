The first five weeks added about 200 followers a week. From 29 Oct to 19 Nov SEVEN added 3,473 in three weeks. The 6 Nov analysis had TikTok behind its 475-a-week plan; it is now well ahead. Set a more ambitious target (15K by end of March is proposed later) so the team keeps pushing.
