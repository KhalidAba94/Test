This is the pattern of paid in-stream and website-card ads. Paid status is still unconfirmed, the tracker has no paid flag, and the X Ads data agreed on 16 Oct hasn’t been shared. Until then, these views can’t be judged on bookings.

Sources: 6 Nov analysis (4 Oct screenshot); YouTube channel RSS feed, 19 Nov 2026 (post register). Six YouTube films account for 99% of the channel’s 8.06M lifetime views.

Paid reach is not bad in itself. The problem is that we can’t tell what it buys. Ask Performance for X Ads and Google Ads data for 30 Sep, 4 Oct, 19 Oct and the August YouTube films: spend, clicks, cost per click and any bookings. Then put a paid flag and spend column in the tracker so paid and organic are never mixed again.
