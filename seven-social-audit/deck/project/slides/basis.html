What this rests on

Four public sources, read together; no data from the accounts’ own analytics

Weekly tracker

27 account-platform rows: SEVEN, Scene, 3 cinemas, 2 malls, 6 attractions and Qiddiya. Friday captures from 25 Sep to 20 Nov. Follower counts re-verified on native profiles on 20 Nov.

6 Nov analysis

Seven weeks to 5 Nov: reach, engagement, content and booking path. It covers 436 logged posts, with screenshot evidence for the key numbers.

20 Nov evidence

27 native profile screenshots and the X feeds for the week to 19 Nov, with SEVEN’s newest posts and their links.

Post register

71 SEVEN posts with screenshots, captured 19–20 Nov: YouTube (45), Snapchat (16) and TikTok (10), including views, likes, comments and shares where shown.

Limits: public views include paid and repeat views, so they aren’t reach. Follower counts round to the nearest 100. Logged-out X shows only about the 5 newest posts. Instagram and Facebook posts sit behind a login. Patterns here are associations in time, not cause.

Sources: SEVEN / Scene weekly social tracker; 6 Nov analysis; 20 Nov evidence archive; seven-social-audit post register.

Each source covers a gap in the others. The tracker gives the time series and peers. The 6 Nov analysis gives post-level X reach and the content read. My register adds TikTok post metrics, Snapchat and the full YouTube history, which the tracker cannot see. None of this replaces native analytics: reach, clicks and spend are still unknown.
