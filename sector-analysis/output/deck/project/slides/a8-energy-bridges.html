Aramco’s realised crude price rose from $71.0 to $91.7 a barrel while liquids output fell from 10.54 to 8.93 million barrels a day; on crude revenue, the price gain outweighed the volume loss by about SAR 40bn. Aramco’s acquisitions and disposals moved revenue by at most 1.6 points in any year. ADES is the opposite: its reported growth is mostly acquisitions (Shelf Drilling rigs and others); excluding them, revenue fell 1.6% in FY2025 and 18.3% in 9M 2026. Like-for-like needs the revenue from newly added rigs or vessels, which the filings do not give, so it is marked not determinable rather than estimated.
