The numbers in the top row are from the final budget approved on 24 November 2026. The table is our interpretation, kept separate from the historical results, and each direction is against 2026. Energy: a rebound in output is net positive only if it lifts output clearly above the 2026 average; if disruption persists at 2026 intensity, 2027 roughly repeats 2026. Banks: more government borrowing from domestic and private channels keeps funding costs up, credit costs could rise if the escalation continues, and the new 15% minimum top-up tax could cost an in-scope bank about 5% of pre-tax profit, depending on rules not yet reviewed.
