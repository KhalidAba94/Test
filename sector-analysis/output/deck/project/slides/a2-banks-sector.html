Net financing income is net special commission income for conventional banks and net income from financing and investments for the four Islamic banks. Costs exclude impairments. ROE deducts Tier 1 sukuk distributions from profit and Tier 1 sukuk from equity. Cost of risk is now on one basis for all ten banks: ECL on loans and financing from each bank’s credit-risk note, net of recoveries, over average net loans. The income-statement line also holds investments and commitments, so it can differ (Alinma FY2025: SAR 1.0bn vs 1.9bn on loans). On the old basis cost of risk appeared to fall steadily (46 to 30 bp); on the comparable basis it bottomed at 32 bp in FY2024 and rose to 35 bp in FY2025. Excluding SNB (the largest by income), income grew 11.5% a year and profit 15.4%, and FY2025 ROE was 15.9%.
