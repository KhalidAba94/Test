A1 · Method

How the numbers are built

Periods

Full years FY2022–FY2025. 9M 2026 (1 Jan–30 Sep) is compared only with 9M 2025, never with a full year. Growth to 2025 is a three-year compound rate; a 2022–26 rate needs FY2026 results.

Latest restated figures

Each year uses the latest filing that shows it. Restated figures are flagged, the original value is kept, and the reason is recorded.

Sector maths

Totals and ratios come from summed company figures, never averages. Growth uses companies with data in both periods; contributions are points of the prior total.

Reported vs like-for-like

Reported growth less acquisitions and disposals, currency, hyperinflation and accounting changes is organic growth; less new capacity, like-for-like. Otherwise “not determinable”.

Not meaningful (n.m.)

Growth and margins are n.m. when the base is negative or below SAR 10m, or when a profit turns into a loss.

Sector measures

Banks: ROE on ordinary equity after Tier 1 distributions; cost of risk in bp. Energy: margins on revenue plus Aramco’s other income related to sales; EBITDA calculated alike for all.

Price, tariff and rate changes in the existing business count as like-for-like; they are not treated as non-organic. The riyal is pegged to the dollar, so currency effects matter only for other currencies.
