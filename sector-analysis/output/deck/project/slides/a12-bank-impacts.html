← Budget summary · Contents

The government plans to borrow more from domestic and private channels; in the first nine months of 2026 gross domestic borrowing equalled about 7.7% of bank deposits, so funding stays tight. Credit costs rose 18% in 9M 2026, two-thirds at Al Rajhi, and could rise further if the escalation since 30 September continues. The top-up tax applies to Saudi profits of banks that are multinational groups (SNB and Al Rajhi have foreign units; others not verified); for a bank paying 10.2% (Al Rajhi), the top-up would be about SAR 1.3bn on FY2025 pre-tax profit, as an order of magnitude only. The per-bank view is an exposure ranking, not a forecast; Riyad, BSF and Aljazira look most exposed.
