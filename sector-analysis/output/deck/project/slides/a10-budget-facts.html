A10 · Budget 2027: facts and signals

Confirmed figures, kept apart from stated intentions

Confirmed: final budget and enacted measures

  • Spending SAR 1,392.1bn, revenue SAR 1,201.5bn, deficit SAR 190.6bn (3.6% of GDP).
  • 2026 revised: deficit SAR 245bn (4.9% of GDP) against 165bn budgeted; real GDP −2.1%.
  • 2027 real GDP +10.9% on an oil-output recovery; no oil-price assumption is published.
  • Subsidies −61.6% to SAR 18bn as electricity and water tariffs rise from 1 Jan 2027.
  • Debt SAR 1,923bn at end-2027 (35.9% of GDP); actual end-Q3 2026: SAR 1,774bn.
  • Also enacted: procurement law, SANED wage support, 1% countercyclical buffer, repo +25 bp to 4.50%, 15% top-up tax, Nitaqat wage floors.

Stated intentions and signals (not yet measures)

  • 2027 borrowing to focus on domestic and private channels; the NDMC plan is pending.
  • Some projects to be rescheduled or restructured; no limit on security spending.
  • A marine insurance pool for shipments to Saudi ports (Minister’s statement).
  • National Privatization Strategy with about 202 projects; no share sale announced.
  • East-West Pipeline to about 10 mb/d under consideration, no final decision; OPEC+ sets 2027 levels on 29 Nov.
