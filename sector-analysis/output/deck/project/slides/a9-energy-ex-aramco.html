So what

Without Aramco, 9M 2026 revenue more than doubled (+125%) and profit swung from a SAR 1.2bn loss to SAR 17.1bn, almost all at Petro Rabigh and Bahri. These are crisis earnings, the most exposed to normalisation in 2027.

Bahri, Petro Rabigh, ADES, Arabian Drilling and SARCO; ROE 9M annualised

Sector averages describe Aramco and say almost nothing about the other five companies. Together they lost money in FY2023 to FY2025, mainly because of Petro Rabigh’s losses, while Bahri and ADES stayed profitable. In 9M 2026 crisis freight rates and refining margins turned the group sharply profitable. Growth rates across a loss are shown as not meaningful in the workbook and the interactive page.
