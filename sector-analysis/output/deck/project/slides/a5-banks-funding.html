The response to rates differs bank by bank, so there is no simple fixed-rate versus floating-rate rule. In the 2023 hiking year six banks gained margin and four lost it; the FY2022 to FY2023 change also reflects the lagged repricing of the 2022 hikes. In the 2024–25 cuts every bank except Al Rajhi lost or held margin. This is why the September 2026 rise of 25 bp is rated “mixed by bank, small”. Loans to deposits is the simple ratio; SAMA’s regulatory ratio uses a different definition and is below the 90% cap at all ten banks. Al Rajhi and SAB deposit series have breaks (reclassifications), noted in the workbook.
