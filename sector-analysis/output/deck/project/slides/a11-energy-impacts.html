← Budget summary · Contents

Directions are against 2026, not against the 2022–25 trend. If the disruption persists at 2026 intensity, 2027 roughly repeats 2026 for Aramco and stays strong for Bahri and Petro Rabigh; only a worsening is clearly negative. The 15% minimum top-up tax matters most for Petro Rabigh: its 9M 2026 zakat and tax was 5.8% of pre-tax profit, and at about 60% Aramco ownership the model rules’ joint-venture provision could bring it into scope, though asset-based relief and normal margins could reduce any top-up to little or nothing. Indicators to watch: monthly Saudi crude output, the OPEC+ decision of 29 November, Brent and tanker rates, Hormuz and Red Sea traffic, and the January 2027 fuel and feedstock price notices.
